Motion Prompt #003

What it is: Lines and dots burst outward as something appears
What the sample shows: A like heart fills in, and a ring, lines and dots burst outward at the same moment.
Spec:
- One burst per 2.35 s. A heart (size 46, 7 px outline) sits in the centre.
- Anticipation: 0.3–0.5 s, scale 1→0.7 (quadratic ease-in-out).
