What it is: Abrupt cut to a contrasting scene
What the sample shows: A quiet scene of a person asleep cuts with no warning straight to an intense one; the volume meter jumps at the same moment.
Spec:

- Top-right readout shows "♪ 静か" (quiet) → "♪ 大音量" (loud)
When to use it: For comedy or shock, showing in an instant the gap between calm and chaos or between expectation and reality.
