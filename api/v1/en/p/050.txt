Motion Prompt #050

What it is: A menu panel slides in from the side of the screen
What the sample shows: Clicking "Menu" slides a tall panel in from the left; clicking outside slides it back.
Spec:
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. The cursor clicks the "Menu" button (110×34, top left) at 1.0 s.
- Open amount a: 0→1 over 1.0–1.38 s with easeOutCubic. Full-card scrim rgba(18,20,28) at opacity 0.36·a.
- Panel: white, 210 wide, full card height, x = −210 + 210·a so it slides in from the left edge. Shadow blur 22, alpha 0.25.
- Inside: an avatar (radius 14) and name at the top, then four menu rows 32 px apart; the first row is selected with a light-blue band.
- Close: the cursor clicks outside at (420, 120) at 3.3 s; a is multiplied by (1 − easeInOutCubic over 3.3–3.65 s) and the panel slides back.
When to use it: Use it to keep navigation or filters off screen until needed, especially on mobile.
