Motion Prompt #064

What it is: Numbered buttons that move between pages
What the sample shows: Clicking 2 and then 3 in the page numbers moves the blue circle and swaps the list above; finally it goes back to 1.
Spec:
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. "‹ 1 2 3 4 5 ›" centred, each 36 in diameter with 8 px gaps, at y 160. A blue circle (radius 18) marks the current page, with white text.
- The cursor clicks 2 at 1.0 s, 3 at 2.1 s and 1 at 3.3 s.
- The blue circle moves 44 px per page over 0.2 s each with easeInOutCubic (88 px at once from 3 back to 1).
- The three-row list above dips in opacity 1 → 0 → 1 within 0.2 s either side of 1.05, 2.15 and 3.35 s, and its content changes at the dip.
When to use it: Use it to split a long list, such as search results or articles, into numbered pages.
