Motion Prompt #069

What it is: Grey shapes stand in for content while it loads
What the sample shows: Clicking reload turns the list into grey blocks of the same shape with a sweeping shine; the content returns when loading ends.
Spec:
- 4.5 s cycle on a 542×191 card. A two-row list (a 96×56 picture and three text lines each). The cursor clicks the reload icon at top right at 1.0 s.
- Over 1.05–1.2 s (linear) the content switches to grey #e9e7e5 rounded blocks of the same size and position.
- Shine: a white band 160 wide (transparent at the edges, opacity 0.75 in the middle) sweeps from x −200 to x 742 every 0.9 s, drawn only over the blocks.
- The content returns over 3.0–3.3 s (linear).
When to use it: Use it for lists or cards that take a moment to load, so the layout is visible while people wait.
