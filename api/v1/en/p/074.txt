Please use "Fade-in on scroll" (滚动淡入), one of the names in "Landing page & web effects".
Motion Prompt #074

What it is: Elements fade in as they scroll into view
What the sample shows: As the page scrolls, the heading, the cards and the body text each rise into place and fade in when they enter the view.
Spec:
- Scroll: 0→95 px at 0.35–1.0 s, 95→200 px at 1.5–2.1 s, 200→0 at 3.2–4.4 s (all cubic ease-in-out).
- Trigger line at 85% of the viewport height (143 px from the top). An element fires when its top edge passes above it (the Intersection Observer idea).
- After firing, over 0.7 s: opacity 0→1 and offset 28 px below → 0 (cubic ease-out).
- Three elements: the heading (172 px down the page), a row of three cards (206 px) and body text (334 px), all below the view at first. The cards in the row appear together.
- When an element leaves the bottom of the view on the way back up, it is reset out of sight and plays again next time.
When to use it: Use it on a long landing page to bring content in piece by piece as the reader scrolls.
