Please use "Preloader" (预加载画面), one of the names in "Landing page & web effects".
Motion Prompt #089

What it is: A screen shown while the page loads
What the sample shows: On a navy screen the number under the logo counts from 0% to 100%; then the screen lifts away and the page underneath fades in.
Spec:
- A navy screen fills the viewport with a logo, a number (28 px) and a bar (180 × 4).
- 0→100% at 0.25–2.1 s (cubic ease-out, rounded to whole numbers); the bar fills in orange at the same rate.
- At 2.3–2.85 s the whole screen moves up 168 px and out (cubic ease-in-out).
- The page heading and photo fade in 0→1 and rise from 14 px below at 2.35–2.95 s (cubic ease-out).
- At 4.25–4.75 s the screen comes back down from the top, at 0% (the next load).
When to use it: Use it on image- or video-heavy sites to turn the loading wait into a brand moment.
