Motion Prompt #091

What it is: More items load automatically at the bottom
What the sample shows: Scrolling down to the end of the list brings up a spinner, then three new items are added so reading can go on.
Spec:
- A list of items (331 × 44, radius 8, 52 apart) with a spinner (radius 10, one turn per 0.8 s) under the last item.
- Scroll: 38→116 px at 0.25–0.85 s, 116→194 px at 1.15–1.75 s (cubic ease-in-out); it stops with the end of the list in view.
- At 2.6 s three items are added: each fades 0→1 and rises from 10 px below over 0.35 s (cubic ease-out). The spinner moves to the new end.
- Each cycle advances by three items (156 px). The content repeats every three items, so the end of the cycle looks the same as the start.
When to use it: Use it for feeds and news lists that people should keep reading without paging.
